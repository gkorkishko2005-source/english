Privacy Policy

PolyGlotty · Last updated: 25 June 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information PolyGlotty ("the Service", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and your choices. We aim to collect only what is needed to run a learning app.

2. Information we collect

  • Telegram account data: your Telegram user ID and the profile information Telegram shares with the app (such as first name and language code) so we can identify your account.
  • Learning data: your progress, levels, vocabulary, hearts, streaks and similar in-app activity needed to deliver the learning experience.
  • Purchase data: records of subscriptions and purchases. Payments are processed by Telegram; we do not collect or store your card details.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide and personalise lessons and track your progress.
  • To manage subscriptions, hearts and other entitlements.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the Service.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers that host the Service, and by Telegram for delivery and payments, solely to operate the Service. We may disclose data where required by law.

5. AI features

Some features send your input (for example, text you ask about) to a third-party AI provider to generate responses. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information through these features.

6. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You may request deletion of your account data through in-app support.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us through the support option in the app.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under the age required by your local law to consent to data processing without parental approval.

9. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

10. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the support option inside the PolyGlotty app.
