# PolyGlotty Terms and Service Rules

Last updated: 2026-05-27

These rules define what PolyGlotty does, what it does not promise, and which situations are outside PolyGlotty's control.

## 1. Service Scope

PolyGlotty is a Telegram WebApp for learning English. It includes a course, flashcards, tests, a learning path, progress tracking, practice tasks, and the subscription-based ALEX AI assistant.

PolyGlotty is an educational product. It is not an official school, university, exam center, legal advisor, medical service, financial advisor, or government institution.

Certificates, levels, XP, streaks, badges, and achievements inside PolyGlotty show progress within the product. They are not official international qualifications, government documents, or accredited certificates.

## 2. Learning Results Are Not Guaranteed

PolyGlotty helps users study consistently, but it cannot guarantee a specific external result.

PolyGlotty does not guarantee:

- admission to a school, university, or program;
- employment, promotion, or salary growth;
- a specific IELTS, TOEFL, school, or university exam score;
- fluent speaking within a fixed period;
- correction of every possible mistake;
- the same progress speed for every user.

Learning results depend on many factors: starting level, study frequency, practice time, memory, motivation, age, device, internet quality, and how the user applies the material outside the app.

## 3. AI Can Be Wrong

ALEX can explain grammar, check texts, generate examples, help with practice, and answer learning questions. However, AI models can make mistakes, miss context, oversimplify, hallucinate, or provide inaccurate wording.

Users should verify important information, especially:

- exam requirements;
- official documents;
- legal, medical, financial, or immigration-related text;
- professional emails and contracts;
- translations where accuracy matters;
- any decision that may have serious consequences.

AI replies are not legal, medical, financial, immigration, tax, or other professional advice.

## 4. Payments and Subscriptions

Digital subscriptions are paid through Telegram Stars. Access is activated after a successful payment and is linked to the user's Telegram ID.

If payment succeeded but access did not appear, the user should contact payment support through `/paysupport` and provide:

- selected plan;
- payment time;
- payment screenshot;
- Telegram ID, if known.

Refunds, disputed payments, regional restrictions, platform fees, taxes, and payment availability are governed by Telegram Stars, Telegram, App Store, Google Play, banks, and other payment providers. PolyGlotty cannot control those third-party rules or decisions.

## 5. Third-Party Services and Availability

Some features may temporarily work worse or become unavailable because of services outside PolyGlotty's control.

This includes:

- Telegram, Telegram WebView, Telegram Stars, or Bot API outages;
- App Store, Google Play, banking, or regional payment restrictions;
- Anthropic, AI model, TTS, STT, hosting, CDN, DNS, or Railway issues;
- weak internet connection, VPN, device limitations, microphone settings, browser settings, or operating system updates;
- Telegram, iOS, Android, browser, or WebView changes;
- API limits, model speed limits, model unavailability, or provider-side safety restrictions.

PolyGlotty will try to restore affected functionality when possible, but it cannot guarantee uninterrupted operation of third-party services.

## 6. Limits, Models, Pricing, and Sustainability

PolyGlotty may change limits, model availability, quota point costs, prices, plan contents, XP rewards, and feature access.

These changes may be needed to:

- keep the product financially sustainable;
- prevent abuse;
- handle server load;
- adapt to AI provider pricing;
- replace unavailable models;
- keep subscriptions fair for paying users.

If a model is temporarily unavailable or too expensive for a plan, PolyGlotty may replace it with a comparable model, adjust quota costs, or temporarily limit the feature.

## 7. Fair Use and Abuse Prevention

Users must not:

- bypass limits or quotas;
- create fake accounts for free access or bonuses;
- automate requests or overload the server;
- scrape, copy, or mass-export product content without permission;
- attempt to receive paid access without payment;
- interfere with APIs, payment flow, database, or security checks;
- send spam, abusive content, illegal content, or harmful instructions;
- impersonate PolyGlotty, its support, or its team;
- present internal PolyGlotty progress certificates as official accredited documents.

Violations may lead to restricted access, cancelled bonuses, removed progress, blocked accounts, or refusal of support for abusive activity.

## 8. User Content and Safety

Users are responsible for the text, voice, files, and other materials they send to PolyGlotty.

Users should not send:

- passwords;
- bank card details;
- passport scans or sensitive documents;
- private conversations;
- medical records;
- addresses;
- third-party personal data;
- confidential business information;
- anything they are not allowed to share.

PolyGlotty may process user messages, voice input, and learning data through AI, TTS, STT, hosting, and database providers only to operate product features.

## 9. Privacy and Data Deletion

PolyGlotty stores data required for learning and subscriptions, including Telegram ID, name or username, interface language, learning level, XP, streak, progress, subscription status, flashcards, mistakes, settings, and learning context.

PolyGlotty does not sell personal data.

To request data deletion, users should contact `/support` and include their Telegram ID. Deleting data may remove progress, personalization, mistake history, learning settings, and subscription-related settings.

## 10. Support

PolyGlotty Support tries to respond quickly, but requests are handled in queue and instant replies are not guaranteed.

App questions: `/support`

Payment questions: `/paysupport`

Telegram Support does not manage purchases or subscriptions inside PolyGlotty.

## 11. Changes to These Rules

PolyGlotty may update these rules, prices, limits, features, and plan contents. The latest version applies after publication in the bot, WebApp, or repository.

By continuing to use PolyGlotty or buying a subscription, the user agrees to the current version of these rules.
